### INV-7 - Every installed question pack must pass the full quality bar (coverage + accuracy + usability)
area: ["question-packs/**/*.json", "question-packs/**/_course.json", "scripts/lint_packs.py", "scripts/hybrid_verify.py", "scripts/verify_pack.py", "scripts/factcheck_pack.py", "scripts/critic_providers.py", "scripts/critic_panel.py", "scripts/certification_campaign.py", "scripts/recert_sweep.py", "scripts/build_manifest.py", "scripts/pack_cert.py", "scripts/lint_hook.py", "scripts/hooks/**", "tests/test_install_gate.py", "tests/test_hybrid_verify.py", "tests/test_certification_campaign.py", "tests/test_critic_providers.py", "tests/test_lint_packs.py", "tests/test_security_plus_final_review.py"]
gate_test: tests/test_install_gate.py
threshold: 3
rationale: Prevents a pack lacking a coverage_blueprint, failing L23 coverage, or lacking a fresh factual certification from being built/installed into the app. Pack quality (accuracy, coverage, Q&A quality) is the project's top priority and must hold for every pack regardless of which agent or human authored it, enforced by project tooling (not any agent-specific hook). There is NO waiver, exception, or fallback path: a pack that does not pass every gate does not install. Four properties are non-negotiable and non-waivable. (1) L25 - every prompt must be answerable from what the learner is shown; a prompt attributing the answer to a chapter, textbook, or study guide they do not have is a critical failure. (2) L26 - no true_false or matching questions; neither format appears on the certification exams this tool prepares for, so they spend a fixed question budget on a shape the exam never tests. (3) L27 - every question in a course must name an `exam_area` declared in that course's `_course.json` `syllabus` block, and the syllabus must cite the published authority it was transcribed from (vendor exam objectives for a certification, the syllabus for a class, or an explicit `kind: "none"`). For `kind: "exam_objectives"`, that citation must be an absolute HTTPS URL and the source must carry `syllabus_verified_by.reviewer` and `.date` attesting an independent objective-alignment review; `kind: "syllabus"` and `kind: "none"` remain exempt. The citation and attestation record where to look and what review was claimed, but do not prove faithful transcription. The taxonomy is external by construction: a pack author who invents the areas measures alignment against their own mental model, which is the thing under test. Referential integrity is the decisive half - an `exam_area` the course does not declare produces a well-formed pack carrying a phantom objective with a near-empty denominator, and per-area weakness ranking (which by construction points at the WEAKEST area) would send the learner straight at the typo; nothing else in the toolchain notices, so a waiver here would readmit the failure with a justification attached and the justification does not make the ranking less wrong. Weight-derived per-area minimums close the self-derived coverage gap by tying required counts to the published syllabus weights. (4) certification must NAME an approved external review method and carry per-question stamps; an unnamed method and an absent stamp registry are failures, not legacy passes. `hybrid_verify.py`, using its configured high-capability verifier profile, is the sole operator path that may write a final certification stamp. The advisory route is resolved at runtime from the approved OpenCode Go low-tier roster and is never certification evidence. Direct `verify_pack.py` and panel primitives remain available only as non-certifying review mechanisms and cannot mint a stamp. A campaign freezes complete high-verifier census evidence against the actual pack snapshot, batches remediation, and requires exact changed-ID rechecks. Its final deterministic stamp reruns Layer A only and must not invoke a fresh reviewer; new concerns begin a new campaign. Discovery evidence must live outside `question-packs` under `.logs/`; the runner rejects an install-tree evidence destination. Evidence that is incomplete, malformed, snapshot-mismatched, or lacks full coverage blocks progression. A finding blocks only for `wrong-answer`, high-confidence factual `misleading-explanation`, or structured multi-defensible-answer `ambiguous`; nits, duplicates, cues, off-axis options, and ordinary option-quality findings remain advisory. Added 2026-08-07 after the SY0-701 pack passed this gate with zero live findings and proved ~59% unusable (54 source-dependent prompts, 61 exam-invalid questions). That failure was a FALSE NEGATIVE from a single critic pass, which is indistinguishable at the gate from a careful pass that found nothing. Independent review evidence remains essential: its findings are unioned for remediation, never removed by majority vote or agreement. A designated final verifier is only trustworthy when its identity and profile are configured by project policy rather than supplied by an arbitrary caller; profile changes therefore require explicit review and re-certification. The certification path records the observed verifier identity and refuses incomplete, malformed, or blocking evidence.

> Scope note (2026-08-07): INV-7 is prospective. Archived/staged packs are historical artifacts and are not reopened or retroactively repaired; the ledger baseline makes those findings null for recurrence. Every future installed pack remains subject to the full gate.

### INV-12 - The native app must study installed packs and never substitute built-in content
area: ["app/QuizzlerKit/Sources/QuizzlerKit/Questions/PackCatalog.swift", "app/QuizzlerKit/Sources/QuizzlerKit/Questions/PackLoader.swift", "app/QuizzleriOS/Launchpad/StudyCatalog.swift", "app/QuizzleriOS/Launchpad/LaunchpadView.swift", "app/QuizzleriOS/TestingSupport/StudyPreviewFixture.swift", "scripts/build_pack_assets.py", "app/project.yml", "tests/test_build_pack_assets.py"]
gate_test: tests/test_build_pack_assets.py; app/QuizzleriOSTests/StudyCatalogTests.swift; app/QuizzlerKit/Tests/QuizzlerKitTests/PackCatalogTests.swift
threshold: 2
rationale: A shipped build once contained three hardcoded questions and never called `PackLoader` at all, while the Today screen printed a fixed position and score as literal text. Nothing failed: the app looked like a working course. Four properties close that class of defect. (1) Question content reaches a screen only through `PackCatalog`, which decodes packs bundled by `scripts/build_pack_assets.py` and verifies each against the `content_digest` recorded in `question-assets.json`; a pack whose bytes no longer hash to its recorded digest is refused, not used. (2) Every pack bundled into a build must satisfy the native metadata contract (lint L29, mirroring `PackManifest.validate()`); a pack the decoder would refuse fails the build rather than shipping as a silently empty course. (3) When no pack loads, the app must show an explicit empty state naming the reason - substituting sample or preview questions makes an empty install indistinguishable from a working one, which is the original defect. Packs that were bundled but refused are reported in Settings rather than dropped. (4) Demo and preview questions live only in files matching the Release `EXCLUDED_SOURCE_FILE_NAMES` patterns, so they cannot be compiled into an archive; the Launchpad must contain no reference to them and no literal question count, position, or course name. Because `question-packs/*/` is gitignored, bundling runs at build time over locally installed packs: two machines with different installed material produce different apps, and the per-pack digest in the manifest is what makes a given build self-describing about the content it carries.

### INV-8 - High-impact exam-course banks require elevated content review beyond the automated gate
area: ["question-packs/**/*.json", "question-packs/**/BUILD_NOTES.md", "scripts/hybrid_verify.py", "scripts/verify_pack.py", "scripts/pack_cert.py", "scripts/critic_panel.py", "docs/VALIDATION_RULES.md", "docs/CRITIC_PROVIDERS.md"]
gate_test: process; recorded in the course's BUILD_NOTES.md (independent review + human judgment cannot be a deterministic test; see rationale)
threshold: 3
rationale: INV-7 certifies each pack as well-formed, covered, and factually stamped only after the frozen hybrid discovery evidence and deterministic campaign finalizer pass; the advisory route is resolved at runtime from the approved OpenCode Go low-tier roster and is never certification evidence. A large, exam-stakes bank (a full multi-pack certification course, or any course whose questions a user will stake a real exam and fee on) carries impact that one probabilistic reviewer run does not fully de-risk. The ship-ready path MUST pass all three, with the outcome recorded in the course's BUILD_NOTES.md: (1) the frozen hybrid discovery census plus deterministic `--certify-campaign` finalization, which blocks wrong answers and high-confidence defects; (2) an independent content + objective-alignment review by a SEPARATE model, covering accuracy of keyed answer + explanation vs the source, coverage/alignment vs the real exam objectives, retention of the strongest question per topic, and difficulty calibration; (3) a human spot-check. THERE IS NO EXCEPTION PATH. The former `codex-local-semantic-review` fallback and its minting script (`scripts/certify_codex_review.py`) were deleted 2026-08-07, along with the `CODEX_REVIEW_METHOD` constant, so the branch cannot be reconstructed from a flag. That fallback let the authoring session certify its own work: `human_spotcheck: "waived-by-David-explicit-cutover-request"` was a hardcoded string constant emitted whenever a CLI flag was passed, so it recorded that a flag was set, not that a human consented. A pack reviewed only by the session that wrote it is not certified. If external reviewer capacity is unavailable, the pack does not ship - it does not ship at a lower assurance level. Direct verifier and panel primitives are non-certifying review mechanisms; neither substitutes for (1), (2), or (3). Complements INV-7 (per-pack automated bar); this is the per-course elevated bar for high-impact banks.

### INV-9 - Shared progress must remain explicit, server-authoritative, and fail-visible
area: ["app/index.html", "app/progress-store.js", "app/shared-progress.js", "app/QuizzlerKit/**", "scripts/serve.py", "scripts/progress_store.py", "scripts/shared_progress.py", "start.sh", "tests/shared-progress.spec.js", "tests/test_progress_store.py", "tests/test_shared_progress_server.py", "playwright.shared.config.js", "docs/ARCHITECTURE.md", "docs/NATIVE_ARCHITECTURE.md", "docs/PROGRESS_PROTOCOL.md", "docs/REPORT_SCHEMA.md"]
gate_test: tests/shared-progress.spec.js
threshold: 3
rationale: Browser-local progress remains the default and the browser stays offline-capable. When shared mode is explicit, CloudKit/server authority must never silently fall back to local writes, accept stale revisions, lose an acknowledged mutation, flatten pack identity, or hide authentication, conflict, persistence, corruption, encoded-size, and network failures from the user. Native sync proposes only the next integer revision in a conditional atomic CloudKit snapshot write guarded by the record change tag and `.ifServerRecordUnchanged`; conflicts require a full fetch/rebase before retry, with no backend or browser private-CloudKit proxy. If a full authoritative fetch proves the zone has no snapshot, recovery resets only the zone-local cursor to revision 0, clears stale operation/issue acknowledgements, retains local facts, and replays by conditional create; concurrent creation still rebases. Operation identity is exactly-once within retention and breaks same-revision recovery ordering only; statuses remain fail-visible. This extension does not weaken INV-5's static, offline, secret-free boundary.

### INV-10 - Course question volume must stay within the learner-workload budget
area: ["question-packs/**/*.json", "question-packs/**/_course.json", "scripts/build_manifest.py", "tests/test_build_manifest.py", "question-packs/AUTHORING.md", "docs/VALIDATION_RULES.md", "README.md", "start.sh", "tests/test_start_sh.py"]
gate_test: tests/test_build_manifest.py
threshold: 3
rationale: Per-pack quality gates do not control the total study burden. The manifest build warns above 200 questions per course and blocks installation above the fixed 240-question ceiling, preventing an exam course from silently growing into a wasteful 400–500-question bank. Course metadata may document a lower target but cannot raise the hard ceiling.
