### INV-7 - Every installed question pack must pass the full quality bar (coverage + accuracy + usability)
area: ["question-packs/**/*.json", "question-packs/**/_course.json", "scripts/lint_packs.py", "scripts/hybrid_verify.py", "scripts/verify_pack.py", "scripts/factcheck_pack.py", "scripts/critic_providers.py", "scripts/critic_panel.py", "scripts/certification_campaign.py", "scripts/campaign_snapshot.py", "scripts/campaign_evidence.py", "scripts/campaign_finalize.py", "scripts/campaign_quarantine.py", "scripts/campaign_inheritance.py", "scripts/issuance_receipt.py", "scripts/campaign_hybrid_adapter.py", "scripts/install_gate.py", "scripts/pack_quarantine.py", "scripts/build_manifest.py", "scripts/pack_cert.py", "scripts/pack_discovery.py", "scripts/hooks/**", "tests/test_install_gate.py", "tests/test_hybrid_verify.py", "tests/test_certification_campaign.py", "tests/test_critic_providers.py", "tests/test_lint_packs.py"]
gate_test: tests/test_install_gate.py
threshold: 3
rationale: Prevents a pack lacking a coverage_blueprint, failing L23 coverage, or lacking a fresh factual certification from being built/installed into the app. Pack quality (accuracy, coverage, Q&A quality) is the project's top priority and must hold for every pack regardless of which agent or human authored it, enforced by project tooling (not any agent-specific hook). There is NO waiver, exception, or fallback path: a pack that does not pass every gate does not install. Four properties are non-negotiable and non-waivable. (1) L25 - every prompt must be answerable from what the learner is shown; a prompt attributing the answer to a chapter, textbook, or study guide they do not have is a critical failure. (2) L26 - no true_false or matching questions; neither format appears on the certification exams this tool prepares for, so they spend a fixed question budget on a shape the exam never tests. (3) L27 - every question in a course must name an `exam_area` declared in that course's `_course.json` `syllabus` block, and the syllabus must cite the published authority it was transcribed from (vendor exam objectives for a certification, the syllabus for a class, or an explicit `kind: "none"`). For `kind: "exam_objectives"`, that citation must be an absolute HTTPS URL and the source must carry `syllabus_verified_by.reviewer` and `.date` attesting an independent objective-alignment review; `kind: "syllabus"` and `kind: "none"` remain exempt. The citation and attestation record where to look and what review was claimed, but do not prove faithful transcription. The taxonomy is external by construction: a pack author who invents the areas measures alignment against their own mental model, which is the thing under test. Referential integrity is the decisive half - an `exam_area` the course does not declare produces a well-formed pack carrying a phantom objective with a near-empty denominator, and per-area weakness ranking (which by construction points at the WEAKEST area) would send the learner straight at the typo; nothing else in the toolchain notices, so a waiver here would readmit the failure with a justification attached and the justification does not make the ranking less wrong. Weight-derived per-area minimums close the self-derived coverage gap by tying required counts to the published syllabus weights. (4) certification must NAME an approved external review method and carry per-question stamps; an unnamed method and an absent stamp registry are failures, not legacy passes. `hybrid_verify.py`, using its configured high-capability verifier profile, is the sole operator path that may write a final certification stamp. The advisory route is resolved at runtime from the approved OpenCode Go standard-tier roster and is never certification evidence; changing its tier does not relax this invariant because certification authority rests on `scripts/verifier_profiles.py`'s registry of reviewed profiles plus the non-certifying call path in `scripts/hybrid_verify.py`, through which only `--certify-campaign` may write a stamp. Direct `verify_pack.py` and panel primitives remain available only as non-certifying review mechanisms and cannot mint a stamp. A campaign freezes complete high-verifier census evidence against the actual pack snapshot and batches remediation into chained rounds, each anchored to the previous round's snapshot with its changed set recomputed from question hashes. Certification reduces to one per-question rule: every question must carry clean high-verifier evidence for its CURRENT content, from either a complete base census that raised no blocking finding on it or a changed-ID recheck in some round that graded it clean at exactly that content. Evidence is bound to the content hash it graded, so editing a question after it was cleared invalidates its evidence and no question can reach a stamp the verifier has not read in its current form; that hash binding, not a declared-change-set comparison, is what makes a chain of rounds a safe substitute for a second full census. A recheck may cover more than its round declared but never less, and a recheck that blocks on one question still clears the others it covered, so a new concern found on a re-read question begins another ROUND rather than another campaign. Its final deterministic stamp reruns Layer A only and must not invoke a fresh reviewer, and records the producing round. Discovery evidence must live outside `question-packs` under `.logs/`; the runner rejects an install-tree evidence destination. Evidence that is incomplete, malformed, snapshot-mismatched, or lacks full coverage blocks progression. A finding blocks only for `wrong-answer`, high-confidence factual `misleading-explanation`, or structured multi-defensible-answer `ambiguous`; nits, duplicates, cues, off-axis options, and ordinary option-quality findings remain advisory. Added 2026-08-07 after the SY0-701 pack passed this gate with zero live findings and proved ~59% unusable (54 source-dependent prompts, 61 exam-invalid questions). That failure was a FALSE NEGATIVE from a single critic pass, which is indistinguishable at the gate from a careful pass that found nothing. Independent review evidence remains essential: its findings are unioned for remediation, never removed by majority vote or agreement. A designated final verifier is only trustworthy when its identity and profile are configured by project policy rather than supplied by an arbitrary caller; profile changes therefore require explicit review and re-certification. The certification path records the observed verifier identity and refuses incomplete, malformed, or blocking evidence. Amended 2026-10-06 (owner-approved): every campaign stamp writes an issuance receipt to its ledger, and a new campaign may inherit a prior campaign's census only through that receipt, at depth 1. The prior must be a current-snapshot campaign that is itself certification-eligible at its frontier, never inherited and never quarantined, and its frontier must equal the new base on every frozen input; any change to subject, source directive, waivers, grounding, verifier profile or model, or pack identity refuses inheritance entirely. Per question, evidence carries over only where the pack stamp equals the receipt's stamp and the question's current content hash, and the full question hash still equals the prior frontier's; every other question gets a tool-computed recheck round, so no question reaches a stamp the verifier has not read in its current form. Inherited evidence is recomputed from the embedded prior ledger and certification block at every read, never from stored qid lists, and the final stamp still reruns Layer A only with no new reviewer call. Residual risk: the ledger is unsigned, so a fully self-consistent forged ledger is no harder to produce than a forged census; the gate defends against drift and partial tampering, not a determined local forger. A quarantine frontier may certify a reduced subset; the held questions are moved out of the pack with a `partial_install` marker, and such a partial pack installs only in Debug builds and is never releasable.

> Scope note (2026-08-07): INV-7 is prospective. Archived/staged packs are historical artifacts and are not reopened or retroactively repaired; the ledger baseline makes those findings null for recurrence. Every future installed pack remains subject to the full gate.

rationale: A shipped build once contained three hardcoded questions and never called `PackLoader` at all, while Today displayed a fixed position and score. Four rules protect question-bank integrity: (1) question content reaches quiz screens only through `PackCatalog`, which decodes packs bundled by `scripts/build_pack_assets.py` and verifies each against the `content_digest` in `question-assets.json`; changed bytes are refused. The case must be labeled synthetic practice and not exam readiness. Because `question-packs/*/` is gitignored, pack bundling uses the locally installed packs; the per-pack digest makes each build self-describing about its question content.

### INV-8 - High-impact exam-course banks require elevated content review beyond the automated gate
area: ["question-packs/**/*.json", "question-packs/**/BUILD_NOTES.md", "scripts/hybrid_verify.py", "scripts/verify_pack.py", "scripts/pack_cert.py", "scripts/critic_panel.py", "docs/VALIDATION_RULES.md", "docs/CRITIC_PROVIDERS.md"]
gate_test: process; recorded in the course's BUILD_NOTES.md (independent review + human judgment cannot be a deterministic test; see rationale)
threshold: 3
rationale: INV-7 certifies each pack as well-formed, covered, and factually stamped only after the frozen hybrid discovery evidence and deterministic campaign finalizer pass; the advisory route is resolved at runtime from the approved OpenCode Go standard-tier roster and is never certification evidence; changing its tier does not relax this invariant because certification authority rests on `scripts/verifier_profiles.py`'s registry of reviewed profiles plus the non-certifying call path in `scripts/hybrid_verify.py`, through which only `--certify-campaign` may write a stamp. A large, exam-stakes bank (a full multi-pack certification course, or any course whose questions a user will stake a real exam and fee on) carries impact that one probabilistic reviewer run does not fully de-risk. The ship-ready path MUST pass all three, with the outcome recorded in the course's BUILD_NOTES.md: (1) the frozen hybrid discovery census plus deterministic `--certify-campaign` finalization, which blocks wrong answers and high-confidence defects; (2) an independent content + objective-alignment review by a SEPARATE model, covering accuracy of keyed answer + explanation vs the source, coverage/alignment vs the real exam objectives, retention of the strongest question per topic, and difficulty calibration; (3) a human spot-check. THERE IS NO EXCEPTION PATH. The former `codex-local-semantic-review` fallback and its minting script (`scripts/certify_codex_review.py`) were deleted 2026-08-07, along with the `CODEX_REVIEW_METHOD` constant, so the branch cannot be reconstructed from a flag. That fallback let the authoring session certify its own work: `human_spotcheck: "waived-by-David-explicit-cutover-request"` was a hardcoded string constant emitted whenever a CLI flag was passed, so it recorded that a flag was set, not that a human consented. A pack reviewed only by the session that wrote it is not certified. If external reviewer capacity is unavailable, the pack does not ship - it does not ship at a lower assurance level. Direct verifier and panel primitives are non-certifying review mechanisms; neither substitutes for (1), (2), or (3). Complements INV-7 (per-pack automated bar); this is the per-course elevated bar for high-impact banks.

### INV-9 - Native progress sync must remain explicit, atomic, and fail-visible
area: ["app/QuizzlerKit/Sources/QuizzlerKit/Sync/**", "app/QuizzlerKit/Sources/QuizzlerKit/Progress/**", "app/QuizzleriOS/Launchpad/LaunchpadProgressModel.swift", "docs/NATIVE_ARCHITECTURE.md", "docs/PROGRESS_PROTOCOL.md", "docs/REPORT_SCHEMA.md"]
gate_test: app/QuizzlerKit/Tests/QuizzlerKitTests/CloudProgressRepositoryTests.swift; app/QuizzlerKit/Tests/QuizzlerKitTests/SyncRecoveryTests.swift; app/QuizzleriOSTests/QuestionShellTests.swift
threshold: 3
rationale: Native sync is the only live shared-progress path. It must never silently fall back to local writes, accept stale revisions, lose an acknowledged mutation, flatten pack identity, or hide account, conflict, persistence, corruption, encoded-size, and network failures. The client proposes only the next integer revision in a conditional atomic CloudKit snapshot write guarded by the record change tag and `.ifServerRecordUnchanged`; conflicts require a full fetch/rebase before retrying. Each new review operation commits its snapshot, operation, and all immutable per-question history events atomically; retries verify the event manifest, and unavailable legacy history is labeled incomplete. If a full authoritative fetch proves the zone has no snapshot, recovery resets only the zone-local cursor to revision 0, clears stale operation/issue acknowledgements, retains local facts, and replays by conditional create; concurrent creation still rebases. Operation identity is exactly-once within retention and breaks same-revision recovery ordering only. Local study remains available offline under INV-5.
